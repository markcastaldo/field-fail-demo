CHOP//STRAND milestone 1 prototype

Note-aware slicing (SCAN) and phase-aligned cuts (SEVER) for bass. Python/numpy, offline. Every number and render below comes from the prototype's test run.

Mostly synthetic, plus three real takes. M1 uses a bass-string model that writes exact ground truth for every note boundary and every fret-noise decoy, so the slicers can be scored. That is the best case. The real bass takes below have no ground truth, so for them the page shows what SCAN and SEVER did and what was measured, not a score.

SCAN: boundary accuracy

  

    
Boundaries matched one-to-one within ±30 ms. Legato = hammer-ons + pull-offs + slide starts.
      The tuning set is what the rules were adjusted on; the held-out set is 10 random takes never looked at during tuning.


  
SEVER: join quality

  

    
E1: 240 random splices inside sustained notes, 31–110 Hz. E2: 150 joins from a note's stable middle into
      another slice that starts mid-waveform (hammer-on, pull-off, slide arrival). "Audible" is a proxy, not a listening test:
      the join stands out as an onset by more than 0.6 dB (a hammer-on scores 0.4–1.0, a soft re-pluck 1.2–2.7) or the level
      dips more than 3 dB. The lowest row is what SEVER now does.

  

    
E3: a 0.4 s note held to 2 s. Wobble = level fluctuation over the held part after removing the trend
      (lower = less audible repetition). Slope = how fast the held note decays; the real note decays at the "natural" rate.
